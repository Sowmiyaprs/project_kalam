## 🎯 **Design Principles (Applied to All Pages)**

### **Color Distribution**
- **85%**: Neutral (white, soft gray) - Base UI
- **10%**: Lavender - Intentional accents (CTAs, focus, active states)
- **5%**: Other colors - Success, error, info, data visualization

### **Typography**
- **Headings**: Neutral 900 (dark charcoal)
- **Body**: Neutral 600 (medium gray)
- **Secondary**: Neutral 500 (muted gray)
- **Links**: Lavender 600 (accent)

### **Components**
- **Cards**: White background, neutral borders, soft shadows
- **Buttons**: Primary = lavender, Secondary = neutral
- **Inputs**: Neutral borders, lavender focus rings
- **Icons**: Neutral default, lavender on interaction

### **Visual Feel**
- Calm and professional
- Elegant and premium
- Balanced and refined
- Modern wellness platform aesthetic
