## 🎨 Design Principles

### Colors
- **Primary**: Soft blue (#0ea5e9)
- **Accent**: Teal (#14b8a6), Purple (#a78bfa)
- **Backgrounds**: White, Soft gray (#f9fafb)
- **Text**: Dark gray (#262626), Medium gray (#737373)

### Typography
- **Font**: Inter (300, 400, 500, 600, 700)
- **Headings**: Semibold, neutral-900
- **Body**: Regular, neutral-600
- **Sizes**: Proper hierarchy (xs to 5xl)

### Spacing
- **Generous whitespace**
- **Consistent padding** (4, 6, 8)
- **Clean margins**
- **Balanced layouts**

### Shadows
- **Soft**: Subtle elevation
- **Medium**: Card hover states
- **Large**: Modals, dropdowns
- **Premium**: Hero sections

### Animations
- **Subtle**: fade-in, slide-up
- **Smooth**: 200-300ms transitions
- **Elegant**: scale-in, hover effects
- **No**: Excessive motion, flashy effects
